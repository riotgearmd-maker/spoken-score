About coverage & voices

This is a growing source-based collection, not yet every opera by every major composer. Each opera identifies its edition and review status. Historical spellings and source variants are retained.

Single Character includes every passage explicitly assigned to that role in the imported source, including shared lines. Unresolved collective labels remain separate; check these and your score before treating a playlist as complete.

Kokoro Nicola is the selected Italian voice after passing an initial doubled-consonant listening test. Its generated passages require a connected Kokoro endpoint. Device speech remains an unreviewed preview, and other languages still require separate voice selection.
